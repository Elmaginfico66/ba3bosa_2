History

Bayern Munich was founded back in 1900 by football enthusiasts from the Schwabing district. What started as a local club has evolved into a powerhouse of modern football. Over the decades, we've managed to establish ourselves as one of Europe's elite teams, constantly competing for titles both domestically and on the continental stage.

The iconic red and white crest

Stadium

Since opening in 2006, the Allianz Arena has been our fortress. Located just north of Munich, this beautiful ground was purpose-built with the modern fan experience in mind. The inflated plastic cushion exterior design is instantly recognizable and can be illuminated in our club colors - a sight that never gets old. With capacity for over 75,000, it's hosted some of the most memorable moments in Bayern history.
